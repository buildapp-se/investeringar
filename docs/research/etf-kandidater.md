## Findings / flags

- **AVWS ISIN**: correct is `IE0003R87OG3` (Avantis factsheet). `IE00034YBzg4` in docs/research/bogleheads-och-internationell-indexforskning.md:205 is wrong (also lowercase letters, not a valid ISIN).
- **Vanguard FTSE Global All-Cap (VGLA/VGLD) is launched**: listed on Xetra, buyable at Avanza and Nordnet, TER 0,07 %, includes small caps. docs/research still calls it "planerad/bevakad" (lagsta-avgifter-och-kostnadsoptimering.md:36, rikatillsammans-indexfonder-och-etfer.md:80, kandidatgenomgang.md:11). It is the direct low-cost rival to SPYI (0,17 %).
- **Xetra tickers differ from forum/London tickers**: FWRA→FWIA, SSAC→IUSQ, SWRD→SPPW, EMIM→IS3N. docs/research uses the London tickers.
- **SCWX fee**: justETF says 0,00 % until June 2026 then 0,17 %; DWS factsheet (2026-08-31) says all-in fee 0,15 %. Issuer figure used. Hybrid/swap replication, Luxembourg domicile.
- **Holdings discrepancies issuer vs justETF**: SPYI 5 998 vs 4 957; IUSQ 1 695 (Jul) vs 2 331 (justETF figure equals index constituent count, likely index not fund). Use issuer numbers.
- **AVWS is active**, not index-tracking, and developed markets only.
- **HSBC ACWI**: not mentioned in any of the six threads, and no HSBC MSCI ACWI UCITS ETF was found in search; excluded. Only HSBC MSCI World (IE000UQND7H4) surfaced.
- **ZPRV/ZPRX/XDEM** are regional or factor tilts, not broad; included because thread 125708/125345 raise them.
- **Countries**: FTSE indexes and Solactive country counts not published in anything read, so UNVERIFIED.
- Thread 106597 also mentions XACT Sverige/Småbolag (Swedish, not global) and PLUS index funds; excluded as out of scope. Thread 58277 is mostly bonds (IS04, UIMB, DBXG, FRC4); no equity candidates added.
